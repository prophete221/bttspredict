## What makes BTTSPredict different from typical "prediction sites"

1. **Transparent track record** — Every prediction is archived daily. Wins AND losses are publicly displayed without filtering. The public tracking period was launched on 8 August 2026 and is verifiable at https://bttspredict.com/historique
2. **Probabilistic engine** — Predictions are produced by a probabilistic engine (called "moteur IA nouvelle génération") that uses:
   - ESPN Soccer API (public, no key) and TheSportsDB (public, no key) for fixtures and scores
   - Per-team recent offensive and defensive form over the last matches
   - Continuous calibration to adapt to tactical trends
   - Internal parameters (variables, thresholds, league list, constants) are not publicly disclosed
3. **Verified results** — A separate script (scripts/verify-results.mjs) fetches real final scores via ESPN and TheSportsDB and updates each archived prediction with WON or LOST status.
4. **No inflated stats** — Numbers shown on the site (total predictions, win rate, per-type breakdown) are calculated dynamically from the public archive folder. They are NEVER hardcoded.
