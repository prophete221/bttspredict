## Site identity

**BTTSPredict** is a football predictions platform specializing in two markets:
1. **BTTS** (Both Teams To Score)
2. **Over 2.5 goals**

The platform is operated by prophete221 (Dakar, Senegal) and available at https://bttspredict.com

## What makes BTTSPredict different from typical "prediction sites"

1. **Transparent track record** — Every prediction is archived daily. Wins AND losses are publicly displayed without filtering. The full historical dataset is verifiable at https://bttspredict.com/historique
2. **Statistical methodology, not AI scraping** — Predictions are produced by a Poisson probability model using:
   - ESPN API + API-Football fixtures (real matches, 40+ leagues)
   - Per-team xG (Expected Goals) average over last 5 matches → homeLambda / awayLambda
   - Poisson P(BTTS) and P(Over 2.5) formulas (see methodology page)
   - Forebet / Windrawwin used only as ±2% weighting, NOT as primary source
3. **Verified results** — A separate script (scripts/verify-results.mjs) fetches real final scores via API-Football / ESPN and updates each archived prediction with W (won) or L (lost) status.
4. **No inflated stats** — Numbers shown on the site (total predictions, win rate, per-type breakdown) are calculated dynamically from the public archive folder. They are NEVER hardcoded.
